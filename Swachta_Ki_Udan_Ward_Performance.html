Circular Economy Lifecycle

How Swachta Ki Udan Operates

A verified 6-step municipal circular waste collection and reward mechanism designed for Mango Municipal Corporation.

01

Segregate at Home

Separate dry clean recyclables (Cardboard, Plastics, Metals, E-Waste) using colour-coded bins.

02

Doorstep Van Arrival

Designated MMC electric tippers arrive at your doorstep in Dimna, Azadnagar, or Jawaharnagar.

03

Digital Scale Weigh-in

Staff scans your Household QR card, weighs scrap with calibrated scales, and records the weight in the terminal.

04

EcoPoints & Thermal Voucher

Residents receive instantaneous EcoPoints in their wallet along with an 80mm thermal receipt code.

05

Depot Bulk Scrap Auction

MMC aggregates collected dry waste at the central depot into verified lots for accredited industrial recyclers.

06

Circular Upcycling Store

Women SHGs in Mango transform sorted paper and fabric scrap into stationery and planters, redeemable with EcoPoints.

Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Mango Municipal Corporation waste initiative

What waste categories qualify for EcoPoints?

Clean plastic bottles/pouches (₹20/kg), dry cardboard and newsprint (₹10/kg), scrap metals (₹30/kg), and discarded household e-waste (₹100/kg). Organic kitchen wet waste is composted and does not receive dry scrap points.

How are EcoPoints calculated and redeemed?

1 EcoPoint equals ₹1 in approved material value. Points can be redeemed for handcrafted upcycled stationery and planters in the reward store or saved for municipal civic incentives.

How does the collection van verify my household?

Every registered household is provided a Digital Household QR code pass. Collection staff scans this QR code at doorstep collection to link measured weights to your account.

Who is permitted to buy bulk depot lots?

Only verified scrap dealers and industrial recyclers registered with Mango Municipal Corporation and possessing a valid GST & Pollution Control Board authorization.
